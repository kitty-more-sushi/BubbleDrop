BubbleDrop sends files between Android phones and Windows PCs over your local network. Nothing goes through the internet, so it uses no mobile data and needs no account.

<b>How it works</b>
Open BubbleDrop on both devices. On the same Wi-Fi they find each other automatically. Pick what to send, tap the other device, and it asks the other side to accept.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Send files, whole folders, photos and videos, installed apps (APK) and text or links</li>
<li>No Wi-Fi? Start a private local-only hotspot from the app. It never uses your mobile data, and a Windows PC can join it from inside BubbleDrop</li>
<li>Connect by QR code, IP address or a network scan when automatic discovery is blocked</li>
<li>Every incoming transfer needs your OK; trusted devices can skip the question</li>
<li>Web Share: any phone or computer can send or download files in a browser with a 4-digit PIN, no app needed</li>
<li>Share from any app through the Android share menu</li>
<li>Keeps receiving in the background, saves to Download/BubbleDrop</li>
<li>Live progress and speed, history, dark and light themes</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
BubbleDrop contacts no server and collects nothing. Transfers stay on your local network. Version 1.x sends files unencrypted over the local network, so use it on networks you trust.

The Windows app is available from the same project page.
